From the Founder

I built Ark & Dove because I watched too many good candidates lose before they started.

I'm Johntel Greene. I've spent over a decade in politics as a registered lobbyist, campaign manager, and technology builder. I've sat in the same chairs you're sitting in now: scrambling to find a website vendor at 11pm, doing calltime in a spreadsheet, paying enterprise prices for tools built for U.S. Senate races when I was running a county race.

Ark & Dove is the answer to a simple question: what if first-time candidates and downballot races had the same integrated stack the big races take for granted, at a price that doesn't bankrupt the campaign? We built it. It's here. Let's win your race.

- Johntel Greene, Founder & CEO

Why "Ark & Dove"

In 1634, the Ark and the Dove carried the first settlers to Maryland's shores. They were small ships with big ambitions, carrying people who believed they could build something new.

That's the spirit behind this company. State and local campaigns are where democracy actually happens, and too many good candidates lose before they start because they can't access the tools and infrastructure the big races take for granted.

We're built by people who've run campaigns, lobbied legislatures, and built political technology from the ground up. We know what works because we've done the work.
